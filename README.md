# The Dead Files

A gritty 1990s UK investigative simulation and tactical point-and-click adventure (Godot 4.x).

You play an overlooked police records clerk who secretly re-investigates cold homicide files,
stakes out suspects, breaks in to secure proof, and decides their fate, all without getting
caught by your own colleagues.
